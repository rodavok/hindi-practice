Sound by sound

Press Play. A pen draws each sound’s colour onto the word while you hear it, in the order the sounds are spoken, so a vowel sign written before its consonant (ि) or an r written on top (र्) is drawn when you hear it, not where it sits. The pen’s path is decorative: it is not the order for writing the letters by hand.

Looking for a voice…

The timing is an estimate: each sound gets a length by type (long vowels longer, the silent a none), and the whole is stretched to the real length of the audio once a word has been heard. If the colours run ahead of or behind the voice, move the slider.
